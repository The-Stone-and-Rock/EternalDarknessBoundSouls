Eternal Darkness: Bound Souls - Windows (64-bit)
================================================

Eternal Darkness: Sanity's Requiem as a native Windows game, for one player or
two on one screen. You need your own image of the European GameCube disc
(GEDP01), as an .iso or .gcm. The USA disc is not supported.

Co-op, character choice, the new graphics options, cheats and save states are
experimental: expect glitches, and please report them.

1. Unzip this folder somewhere you can write to, such as Documents or a Games
   folder (not Program Files).
2. Put your disc image in the "dump" folder. Any file name works.
3. Double-click play.bat. If Windows says it protected your PC, click
   "More info", then "Run anyway". A black window stays open behind the game
   while it runs; that's normal.

Two players: plug in two controllers (or one and the keyboard), then on the
launcher open Co-op, turn on Split-screen co-op, and pick each player's
character under Choose Character.

Separate rooms (new, experimental, co-op only): each player can go through
doors on their own and play in a different room from the other, each half of
the screen showing that player's own room. Solo play is the game's own.
Quick-save states made in co-op won't load solo, or the other way round;
memory card saves do.

The game's program comes ready-made in this folder; its graphics, sound and
music come from your disc. The first start takes a second or two longer while
it reads what it needs from the disc.

HD textures (recommended)
-------------------------
Download both, unpack them (7-Zip opens the .7z file), and put the two folders
in a folder called "textures" in this folder.

- Eternal Darkness HD Texture Pack by Zombie (DDS, 2.9 GB .7z):
  https://drive.google.com/file/d/1KGEr7dF2onTdzUFNp7Lia3p0D0I2-TaZ/view
- PAL supplement, the European disc's screens and words the pack misses
  (DDS, 13.2 MB .zip):
  https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BuoE0uL4K3xn5KfH8KYYtC6fk-6QiiiA/view

Leave both folders as they are; the supplement wins where they overlap.
Options > Graphics > Image > HD textures turns them on and off.

Saves, settings and logs stay in this folder (saves\, settings.ini,
ed-*.dolcard, logs\). To update, copy a new build over this folder: your
saves, settings, disc and textures stay. If something goes wrong, send the
files in saves\reports\ and logs\play.log with a few words on what you were
doing.
