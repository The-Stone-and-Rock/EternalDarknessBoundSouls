## hazards
- Player 2's own sparkles (if they wear a ghost form while apart) get +56 = the room in play when the queued request is consumed, which is player 1's room unless the queue drains inside their turn (coop_stay_room_in): under the per-room rule their orbs would leave their own half and show in player 1's. Fix in ghost_sparkles_step: once g_sparkles[player] is found, write the player's room (coop_object_room of their node) into effect+56 each frame beside the centre move (hle.c:20365-20367). And the step 0x8018ED60 ends an effect whose +56 != r13-23276 when its particles are all spent (0x8018EF58-0x8018EF7C): player 2's effect may die and be remade every 30 frames (g_sparkles_wait) while apart: harmless flicker at worst, worth a log line.
- Don't mark the identity with 3 bytes: 0.0f with a low byte set is a denormal; the recompiled GXLoadPosMtxImm (0x8021FBAC -> 0x8021FB20) may copy with psq and flush it. Use the fade's whole words (hle.c:3345-3348).
- Rows 60-62 must be restored after every effect or every later draw with GXSetCurrentMtx(60) (other sprite kinds, menus) collapses in one half; restore on the !ok path of hle_guest_call too (main.c:88-93 ends the call on an interrupt).
- The marked 27 copy before a kind that draws with 60 is wasted but harmless; the cost is 4 guest matrix loads per effect per frame (tens of effects at most).
- hle_guest_call must not be entered while a GXCallDisplayList continuation is active (hle.c:2286-2289); 0x8017C1D8's entry is outside any (the effect's own GXCallDisplayList at 0x8017C9B0 runs inside the wrapped call, like the room sections').
- The whole-frame moments: when the frame is drawn whole into one half (g_room_whole_owner != 0, hle.c:13676-13690) the mark does nothing (the frame isn't built per view); mirror room_section_draw: skip the draw (return without the guest call, passthrough false) for an effect whose room isn't the owner's.
- The orbs' corners are computed from 0x8060248C, player 1's camera, so in player 2's half they still face player 1's camera (docs/coop-glitch-todo.md U4): a separate, known issue; this change only hides them from the other player's half.
- hle.c's room-section mark is uncommitted work (HEAD 6b2a584 lacks it); build on the working tree.
