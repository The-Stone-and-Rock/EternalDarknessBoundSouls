# Sound facts from the first readers (2026-11-11 afternoon). Verified claims with EVIDENCE lines; treat the 'open' list as open.
Sound in this recomp is ONE guest-side MusyX engine (CPU sequencer and voice engine run in guest RAM, the DSP skipped, hle.c:4454-4466) feeding ONE host mixer (audio.c:193-212) plus ONE host-decoded DTK disc-music stream (dtk.c, hle.c:1312-1352). Sound data is game-wide, not per room: three MusyX banks /audio/effects/ed_fx|ed_boss|ed_menu (.poo/.sam/.pro/.sdi, strings 0x8023EB40-0x8023EC74, opened at sound init 0x8019E96C), 731 MusyX disc streams audio/stream/NNNN in 4 slots (0x805DB5C0, 388 B each, started by 0x801A4884), and 166 DTK names at 0x8024C8E0 (index 0 /audio/music/pious48k.adp), one DTK stream at a time (r13-27080; a second request is dropped). A room's sound is chosen by its scripts, not by a room table: fn168(track,fade)/fn196 set the music program (table 0x80254F98, entries {op u8, param u8, arg u16}; op 1 = play .adp stream arg at volume param), fn126/fn148/fn149 start positioned disc streams, fn47/fn61/fn62 play effects. The room change 0x80152298 does three sound things when the room differs: 0x801A0EAC drops stream-backed tracks, 0x801A05EC(10,1,0) fades all 80 tracks (0x805D80C0, 152 B each) over 10 ticks, 0x80042910 stops the background thread; on arrival 0x80151FDC resets sound groups 0 and 1 to 100 (0x8019E068). It never touches the DTK music, which persists until a script's fn168/fn56. There is ONE listener: every sound tick (0x8019DC50, from the main loop 0x80008500) 0x8019EDD4 copies the camera eye/target (0x801EB01C/0x801EB064) into 0x805D8000+144.. and calls sndUpdateListener (0x801BD7B4, its only call site 0x8019EE98); that camera block is player 1's in the main loop (hle.c:15084-15086). Falloff for positioned sounds/streams (0x801A01D0) takes the better of the camera eye and the controlled character r13-26524. Positioned tracks are tagged with the room r13-23276 at start (+132, dis 0x8019F1E4) and fn52 filters by that room. Per player today: both players' move/footstep sounds play (animation sound keys 0x8019E240; player 2's enchanted swing via coop_move_sound), player 2's torch crackle slot is swapped (coop_torch_swap), the MusyX voice-allocation words r13-22920..-22396 are deliberately never put back (coop_words_back), and the Tome copy's sounds are never heard (voices live in guest memory, 0x8019DB90 not forwarded). Non-positioned effects (0x8019DB90 -> sndFXStartEx(id, vol, pan 63, 0)) are centered and heard by both. Pious (level 1): script 460 sets track 20 at the start, whose program has no .adp (ops 6,2,4: silence/loop [inf]); the chapter's ambience is MusyX disc stream 614 (L01/2116 fn126(614,100,1,3,place)); doors L01/29 and L01/119 play fn62(0,238,60,235,100) after fn66.

## hard
- One listener at one camera: sndUpdateListener is fed from the live camera block every sound tick (0x8019EDD4 -> 0x801BD7B4), and that block is player 1's in the main loop (hle.c:15084-15086). Positioned sounds and disc streams in player 2's room would be attenuated against player 1's eye and r13-26524 (0x801A01D0), i.e. mostly silent or wrong; the same mechanism already makes the A1 pop when r13-26524 is swapped (docs/coop-glitch-todo.md:20).
- One DTK music stream (r13-27080, r13-22904; a second fn54 is dropped, host dtk.c has one stream: hle.c:1312-1352, primitives.tsv fn54). Two rooms whose scripts set different tracks (fn168, 78 scripts, `if fn196() ~= N`) fight over it; one player's fn56/fn168 fade cuts the other's music. The music is also chosen only by scripts (no room table), so whichever player's room scripts run last wins.
- A room change silences the whole room: 0x80152298 drops stream-backed tracks (0x801A0EAC) and fades all 80 tracks over 10 ticks (0x801A05EC(10,1,0)); fn66 fades sound at departure (transitions.md:77); 0x80151FDC resets groups 0 and 1 (0x8019E068). One player's door would cut the other player's room's sounds unless those calls are filtered by owner.
- Tracks are tagged with the room r13-23276 at start (0x8019F104 +132) and fn52/fn195 act on tracks whose room equals the current room (primitives.tsv fn52): with the room word swapped per player, scripts' volume changes and matches hit the wrong set.
- 4 MusyX disc-stream slots (0x805DB5C0, 388 B each, priority at +377): two rooms' ambiences (fn126/fn148), sanity streams (SanitySndRgn) and voice lines (ed64 via 0x801A4884) compete for them, and 0x801A4FCC clears all four at a Tome's opening or a level end.
- MusyX's voice allocation lives in r13-22920..-22396 inside the 2060-byte words block; coop_words_back must never put it back (hung two-part sound, hle.c:11909-11925). Any per-player or per-room swap of game memory has to leave the whole MusyX state (0x805D8000 block, tracks 0x805D80C0, streams 0x805DB5C0, ARAM) as one.
- Voices live in guest RAM and ARAM, driven by the sound tick 0x8019DC50 from the main loop: a second resident room kept in a memory copy (the Tome copy's pattern) is never heard (coop-two-tomes-design.md:160; hle_guest_call polls no interrupts, level3-design.md:294).
- 0x80042910 stops the world's background thread (queue 0x805B2AFC) and waits on every room change and every Tome opening (hle.c:4629-4631); two players changing rooms close together serialize on it [inf].
- Door sounds are the departing player's script (fn62(0,238,60,235,100) after fn66 in L01/29, L01/119) through the centred 0x8019DB90: both players hear every door, as they do every Tome and words sound.
- Sanity: one tracker, one clock, one output; TV Mute mutes everyone, sanity music is the one DTK stream (sanity.md:227,231; mods-feasibility.md:26,38).

## have
- The host owns the final mix and the DTK stream with separate left/right volumes (audio.c:193-212, dtk.c:159-170) and already plans a left/right split of sanity music (sanity.md:227): per-player muting or panning of the music, and a per-side mute of the game mix, are host-side changes.
- coop_be_player2 makes player 2 the controlled character r13-26524 for a game call (coop_move_sound hle.c:12771-12795 does it for their swing's sound), so a sound's falloff, voice choice (ed64) and fn194 position can be computed as theirs.
- The room is an argument of the positioned sound start (0x8019F104's 8th arg, stored at track +132) and tracks are walked by room (fn52) and by group (fn195, 0x805D7C90 groups with 0x8019E068): a natural per-room/per-side filter and ducking already exists in the game.
- tw_keep already intercepts the Tome's sound-hush calls by return address (0x801A0F30, 0x801A05EC, 0x801A4FCC, 0x8019E068 at lr 0x80111134-0x8011115C, hle.c:13352-13356) and passes them over for player 2's copy; the same intercept pattern fits a room change's 0x801A0EAC/0x801A05EC(10,1,0)/0x8019E068 when the change isn't the listener's room.
- Hooks on sndFXStartEx (0x801AD078) and the stream start 0x801A4884 exist (sanity_sound_notify, sanity_stream_notify, hle.c:22263-22275, 22955-22956): a place to tag or refuse sounds by owner or room.
- coop_torch_swap shows the per-player sound-slot pattern (r13-25468 voice/sound/volume swapped, hle.c:12527-12562); coop_words_back knows exactly which MusyX words to leave alone (hle.c:11915-11925).
- Quick states already carry AUDI, DTK and ARAM (quickstate.c:112-113, 385), so audio survives the save/load the tests use.
- Measurement is built: ED_AUDIO_LOG counts every MusyX API call and reports at exit (hle.c:4666-4776), ED_PCM_OUT records the mix (audio.c:226-240), ED_ROOM_LOG names who asked for a room (hle.c:11108), ED_STREAM_LOG every streamed read (hle.c:11293-11298), ED_ARAM_LOG every ARAM transfer (hle.c:975-985).
- The stream name table (0x8024C8E0) and the music program (0x80254F98) decode from generated/main.dol, so every chapter's track -> .adp mapping can be listed without running the game (done here for tracks 3, 20, 21, 26).
- The game's own listener update is one small function (0x8019EDD4) with one caller (0x8019DC64) reading one camera block: a second listener position (or an alternating one) has one place to go.

- Whether the disc-stream feeder (the background thread 0x805BBB60 that 0x80042910 stops) also serves voices and door sounds (hle.c:457-465 says disc streams carry voices and effects), i.e. whether two rooms' loads would stall each other's streams.

## facts
- [engine] MusyX's CPU-side sequencer and voice engine run unchanged in guest memory; only the DSP task boot is skipped and host audio.c executes the resulting AX command lists. __OSInitAudioSystem, __AI_SRC_INIT are no-ops.
    EVIDENCE: runtime/host/hle.c:4454-4468 (kAddrHandlers comments), hle.c:925-927 hle_salInitDsp -> audio_skip_dsp_init
- [mixer] One host mixer: each AID chunk is 8 stereo frames of the game's own mix (effects, voices, sequenced music) scaled by g_settings.effects_volume, then the DTK disc music is mixed in with its own left/right volume scaled by g_settings.music_volume.
    EVIDENCE: runtime/host/audio.c:193-212; runtime/host/dtk.c:159-170 dtk_mix(volume_left, volume_right)
- [music (DTK)] Disc music is a DTK stream: the game calls DVDPrepareStreamAsync on an .adp file and the host (dtk.c) decodes it from the ISO; one stream is active at a time (dtk_active, dtk_prepare, dtk_cancel).
    EVIDENCE: runtime/host/hle.c:1312-1352; main.c:760 dtk_init(iso_path)
